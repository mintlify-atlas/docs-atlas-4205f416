Parkontrol is a comprehensive parking management system that gives your company complete control over every aspect of your parking operations — from space-level tracking to electronic invoicing.

Whether you run a single parking lot or manage multiple facilities across your organization, Parkontrol provides the tools your team needs to operate efficiently.

## Who uses Parkontrol

- **Administrators** — Manage companies, configure parking lots, set rates, view reports, and oversee all operations
- **Operators** — Handle day-to-day operations: check vehicles in and out, manage reservations, and process payments

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy the backend">
    Set up the NestJS API server with your Oracle Database connection. See [Backend Setup](/deployment/backend-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the frontend">
    Configure and run the Angular application pointing to your API. See [Frontend Setup](/deployment/frontend-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your company and admin account">
    Register your organization and set up your first Administrator user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your parking lot">
    Add your parking lot, define cells, assign sensors, and set rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start accepting reservations">
    Operators can log in and begin managing vehicles and payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>
