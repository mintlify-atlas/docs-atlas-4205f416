This guide walks you through the essential steps to get Parkontrol running and process your first vehicle reservation end to end. You will set up the platform, configure a parking lot, define rates, and complete a full check-in and payment cycle.

## Prerequisites

<Note>
  Before you begin, make sure you have the following in place:

  - **Node.js 18 or later** installed on your machine
  - **Oracle Database** instance accessible (host, port, SID, credentials)
  - **Backend running** on `http://localhost:3000` — see [Backend Setup](/deployment/backend-setup)
  - **Frontend running** on `http://localhost:4200` — see [Frontend Setup](/deployment/frontend-setup)
  - An **Administrator** account created during registration
</Note>

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the platform">
    Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:4200/login`.

    Enter your Administrator credentials and click **Ingresar**. Upon successful login, the application reads your role from the JWT token and redirects you to the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).

    <Tip>
      If you do not have an account yet, go to `http://localhost:4200/registro` to register your organization and create the first Administrator user.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first parking lot">
    In the sidebar, click **Parqueaderos**. This opens the parking lot management screen, accessible to both Administrators and Operators.

    1. Click the **Nuevo** (or **+**) button to open the creation form.
    2. Fill in the parking lot name and associate it with your company.
    3. Click **Guardar** to save the parking lot.

    The new parking lot appears in the list immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add cells to the parking lot">
    Navigate to **Celdas** in the sidebar. Cells represent individual parking spaces within a lot.

    1. Click **Nueva celda**.
    2. Select the parking lot you just created.
    3. Choose the cell type (e.g., standard, covered, accessible).
    4. Optionally assign a sensor code to the cell for physical sensor integration.
    5. Click **Guardar**.

    Repeat this step for each parking space you want to track. Each cell starts with the status **LIBRE** (free) and transitions to **OCUPADA** (occupied) when a reservation is created.

    <Tip>
      Create all your cells before setting rates. Rates are tied to vehicle types, so having your lot fully configured first makes the next steps cleaner.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set rates for vehicle types">
    Go to **Tarifas** in the sidebar. This section is restricted to Administrators.

    1. Click **Nueva tarifa**.
    2. Select the **vehicle type** (e.g., car, motorcycle, truck) the rate applies to.
    3. Choose the **billing period** (e.g., per hour, per day).
    4. Enter the price for that period.
    5. Click **Guardar**.

    Define at least one rate for each vehicle type you expect to park. Rates are used during reservation finalization to calculate the total charge.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first reservation">
    Go to **Reservas** in the sidebar. Both Administrators and Operators can create and manage reservations.

    1. Click **Nueva reserva**.
    2. Select the **parking lot** and an available **cell** (status: LIBRE).
    3. Enter or select the **vehicle** (license plate and vehicle type). If the vehicle does not exist yet, add it via **Vehículos** first.
    4. Confirm the entry time (defaults to the current time).
    5. Click **Guardar** to open the reservation.

    The reservation is created with status **ABIERTA** and the cell transitions to **OCUPADA**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finalize the reservation and process payment">
    When the vehicle is ready to leave, locate the open reservation in the **Reservas** list.

    1. Click the reservation to open its detail view.
    2. Click **Cerrar reserva** to record the exit time. The system calculates the total amount owed based on the elapsed time and the applicable rate.
    3. The reservation status changes to **CERRADA**.

    Then navigate to **Pagos** to process the payment:

    1. Find the closed reservation in the payments list.
    2. Select the **payment method** (cash, card, or bank transfer).
    3. Enter the amount and click **Registrar pago**.

    Once payment is registered, you can navigate to **Facturación** to generate and view the electronic invoice for the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>
